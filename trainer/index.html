Live feed needs a Chromium-based browser. It reads the keyboard's USB serial port (left half) through WebSerial, which only Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi and Opera ship, on desktop, over https or 127.0.0.1. Open this page in one of them to see your real key presses and layers on the board. Everything else in the trainer works here.
